Why it works

Tie yourself to the mast.

Odysseus knew he couldn’t resist the Sirens’ song. So before his ship sailed past them, he had his crew tie him to the mast, and told them not to untie him, whatever he said.

Mast works the same way. You decide when you’re calm. It holds you when you’re not.

Held, gently. You won’t drift.

Here’s how it holds you.

When it’s time to focus.

The meeting you blocked for deep work starts. Mast locks the apps you chose until it ends, on your phone and your computer.

When it’s time to sleep.

You meant to stop at 11. At your bedtime the computer turns itself off and the phone goes quiet until morning.

When it’s time to pray.

If you pray, your phone and computer lock a few minutes after each prayer time. Go, and come back to them.

When you really need out.

Ask a friend you chose. When they say yes, you’re out. Or use your one emergency a week. There’s no setting to switch off in a weak moment.

Good to know.

What does Mast lock?

The apps you choose on your phone, or all of them, and your computer too. At the times you set, until they end.

No. Prayer locks are there if you pray. If you don’t, you won’t see them.

Which devices?

iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac. It works best with your phone and your computer together.
