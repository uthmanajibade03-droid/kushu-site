How it works

  1. Choose the apps that pull you away, in the Kushu app.
  2. Tap your phone to Kushu. Your reading session begins, and those apps close. Your computer can lock too.
  3. Read. When the time is up, everything opens again by itself.

Until it’s here

You don’t have to wait. In the Kushu app, the barcode on something you already own (a jar in the kitchen, a book on the shelf), a QR code or an NFC tag can be your unlock object, under Settings → Unlock objects. Keep it away from where you read, and ending a session early means going to get it.
