Using Mast.

Last updated 28 October 2026

Mast locks your phone and computer at the times you set, so you keep to your own schedule. These are the terms for using it, in plain words. By using Mast you agree to them.

Your account

  • You sign in with Google or Apple. Keep that account safe: whoever can sign in to it can use your Mast.
  • You need to be at least 13 to have a Mast account.
  • You can delete your account at any time, from You in the app. Your locks, friends and history go with it.

Locks are your choice

  • You choose what locks and when. Mast is built to hold you to that: a change that makes a lock easier waits a day, and so does signing out while locks are set.
  • Plan around your locks. Don’t set one over something you can’t miss, like work you’re on call for or directions you need. For a real emergency there’s one emergency unlock a week, and your friends can let you out.
  • The friends you add can let you out. Only add people you trust, and only people who agree to it.

Paying

  • Mast is $7.99 a month for your phone and computer together, after 14 days free.
  • The free days start with a card. You’re charged when they end unless you cancel before.
  • You can cancel at any time, from You in the app, before you’re charged again. Payments go through Stripe, under its terms too.
  • If the price changes, we’ll tell you before it applies to you.

Fair use

Don’t use Mast to lock, watch or control someone else’s devices without their say, and don’t try to break, copy or resell it.

What we can’t promise

We work to keep Mast running and your locks on time, but it’s provided as it is. Phones and computers can stop Mast (an update, a setting, no battery), and we can’t promise every lock will always come. As far as the law allows, Mast isn’t responsible for what happens while a lock is on or off, and our responsibility to you is no more than what you paid us in the last 12 months.

Ending

You can stop using Mast and delete your account whenever you like. We may close an account that breaks these terms, and we’ll tell you why.

Changes and questions

If these change, the date at the top will too, and we’ll say so in the app for big changes. Questions are welcome at hello@usemastapp.com.
