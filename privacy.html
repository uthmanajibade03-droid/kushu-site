Privacy

Your reading is yours.

Last updated 1 October 2026

Kushu is built to keep you off your screens, not to watch them. Most of what it knows stays on your phone and your computer. This page says what is kept, where, and why.

Your account

Kushu asks you to sign in with your email. Your account ties your phone and your computer together, so a reading session or a schedule on your phone can lock your computer. For that, Kushu keeps these on its servers:

  • Your email address, to send you a sign-in code. There is no password.
  • Your devices: a name for each (such as “Windows PC”), whether it is a phone or a computer, its platform, and when it was last seen.
  • Your reading sessions: when each started and ended, how it ended (finished, or ended early), whether it started by hand or on a schedule, and whether it should lock your computer. Not what you read.
  • Your reading schedules: each one’s name, time, days, length and time zone.
  • Locks you start from your phone: when each starts and ends.
  • A recitation you share between devices: while you play the Qur’an on one device and have it play or show on the other, the verse range, the verse being recited, the reciter and the playback settings. It is replaced each time and holds nothing once you stop.

Each account can read only its own records. We don’t sell them, share them for advertising, or use them for anything but making Kushu work between your devices.

What stays on your devices

  • The apps you block. On iPhone they are chosen through Apple’s Screen Time, and Kushu never sees which apps they are. On Android the list stays on the phone.
  • Time in apps on your computer, your daily limits and the floating pill: counted and kept on the computer only.
  • Where you are in the mushaf, your recent pages and your reading history.
  • Your prayer settings, sounds and announcements.

Services Kushu uses

  • Supabase stores the account records above.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in email with your code.
  • Prayer times. The computer app works out your city from your internet address, using a location lookup service (ipwho.is, GeoJS or ipapi.co), then asks AlAdhan for that day’s times. It keeps the city, not the address.
  • The Qur’an. Verses, translations, the mushaf’s fonts and recitations are loaded from Qur’an sources (alquran.cloud, Quran.com, QuranicAudio and EveryAyah) and kept on your device. These requests carry nothing about you.
  • Updates. The computer app checks GitHub for new versions.

Kushu has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Sign out to stop your devices sharing anything. To delete your account and everything kept with it, open Settings → Account → Delete account in the Kushu app; it is deleted at once and can’t be undone. You can also email hello@kushu.space from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it. Deleting Kushu from a device removes what it kept there.

Children

Kushu accounts are not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly keep their data.

Changes and questions

If this changes, the date at the top will too. Questions are welcome at hello@kushu.space.
